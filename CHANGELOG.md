# 4.2.0.18

### Mob Farmer
- Optional: Prioritize pots during timed hunt — a Magic Pot can still interrupt the route
- Fights back if you take aggro while gathering, buffing, or stacking
- No longer sits idle when already in combat and “Only start out of combat” is on

### Illegal Mode
- Pausing auto treasure hunt for a CE waits until you are out of combat (no stop mid-fight)
- Optional: Leave mid-FATE for a CE (same timing as leaving while walking to a FATE)
- Optional: walk to the Forked Tower pad when registration opens (Config → Forked Tower); turns off once you are inside
- Forked Tower registration countdown now shows on North Horn too

### Treasure & Carrot Hunt
- Optional: Scout without Fortune Carrots — walk the route and skip spots that need an item
- Carrot Hunt can Pause / Resume like Treasure Hunt
- Pausing Treasure Hunt restores your previous gearset after Ninja Hide
- Less mount / dismount looping near packs or when almost at a coffer (Ninja Hide)
- Keeps the wind-safe approach to the Suspended Masonry coffer (no longer cuts the path short when the chest looks closer)

### Raise
- Optional: Auto-accept raises when someone revives you in Occult Crescent (Config → Raise)
- Optional: Say thanks in /say once you are back up (message is editable)

### Fixes
- Pots & Treasure keeps finishing a live Magic Pot even if it is past Skip FATE progress %
- Auto shopping backs off when the shop helper is busy or refuses to start, instead of retrying endlessly
- Pot chest farm fights back if you take aggro while opening, and only opens chests at pot spots (not nearby map coffers)
- Return to camp and shopping should stutter less while casting Return
