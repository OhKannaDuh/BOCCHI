# 4.2.0.19

### Mob Farmer
- Now works on non-tank jobs: your rotation opens the fight once you reach a mob, instead of standing next to it doing nothing

- Repairing at the camp mender works again (it used to walk up and never open the repair menu)
- Self-repair and dismounting before a repair no longer silently fail
- Steps that wait and retry (repairs, teleports, chests) behave more reliably
- Old config backups are cleaned up automatically; only the latest one is kept
